# Keeper Ulde

Toll-keeper of the weir-gate, the only dry crossing into the lower fen. Squat, patient,
pleasant the way a ledger is pleasant. The first thing a traveller notices: he turns a coin over
his knuckles, always, and never once looks at it. Stands at [[The Weir-Gate]], answers to
[[The Weir-Keepers' Charter]], carries the theme [[What a Fair Price Is]].

<!-- Two lines of what a PC perceives first, then links: place, affiliation, theme — the minimum

## Why it is here
The Charter posts a keeper wherever the fen takes more than it gives. Ulde asked for this gate —
the year his brother stopped answering letters — and the Charter, which never wastes grief,
let him have it.

## What it represents
Not "a greedy official": the mechanism is **complicity priced as duty** — the way an institution
lets a man stand guard over his own family's crime and calls the arrangement a wage.

## The question it poses
He knows what hangs under the bell-house and has said nothing for a year. Is a year of silence a
debt he can still pay — and who is owed, the fen or the dead?

## Playable intention
- **Wants (surface):** the toll, in the form that costs the payer most — he says so, pleasantly.
- **Beneath it:** a witness to the bell-house, so the secret stops being his alone.
- **Surfaces as:** "Everything crosses. Everything pays. The only question is the coin." — and
  the coin he never looks at.
- **If they stall / walk away / contradict him:** he prices things they did not offer / lets them
  go and opens a sluice one finger / agrees too quickly — the tell.

## At the table
Low voice, no haggling tone — he states prices like weather. One repeatable gesture: the coin.
Wry register admitted, but his jokes are all about debts.

## Connections
Brother of the body under [[The Bell-House]] — see GM section. Reports to
[[The Weir-Keepers' Charter]]. Parallel to Tobit's guild-expulsion hook: **whose nerve** — Tobit;
**which emotion** — the shame of a debt honestly incurred; **which question** — whether paying
what you owe ever finishes. Staged as a parallel, not a cameo: Ulde is the same mechanism
wearing another face, and the player makes the connection himself.

## Mechanics
Local trait, if pressed to violence: he fights like a man guarding a queue — threshold 9 to get
past him, and he aims at burdens, never at people. Full block: [[Weir-Keeper — stats]].

## GM only — not player-facing
He hung nothing and cut nothing, but he ferried the rope. The year of silence is the crime he
can be forgiven; the rope is the one he cannot. Reveal: never volunteered — only if the party
brings him proof from the bell-house, and then he tells all of it, at once, unprompted.
