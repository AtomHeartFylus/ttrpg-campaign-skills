## What actually happened
- Tobit paid the toll with a true regret — his guild expulsion, told at the table for the first
  time — instead of coin. **Deviation from prep:** nobody attempted the ledger-read.
- Ulde's "agrees too quickly" tell landed; Bruno called it aloud in character. *(carried)*
- Ferry: Brenna's beat played as written, but with Sorrel absent she returned the question to
  Iole. Cleo answered with the drowned sister — the table went quiet. *(carried)*
- Maren kept her promise to the eel-catcher aloud on the water and regained 1 Wick. Ada was
  otherwise chorus tonight, by rotation, as planned.
- Bell-house: Iole dove without the rope plan; one failure, one wound from the cold. The Wight's
  hesitation at the ledger page was noticed and used as cover.
- **Deviation:** they cut the bell loose *before* identifying the body. Tobit found the
  guild-mark afterwards, on the Wight's still hands, and the players named the brother
  themselves — unprompted.
- Whether the guild-mark matches one Tobit has seen before **(unconfirmed — the round cap was
  reached before this was settled)** — raised at the table but never answered.
