> [!example] Mechanics here
> Reading Ulde's ledger unnoticed: 3d6 vs threshold 9 (7 if Tobit invokes his guild-name). Toll: two marks of coin · a true regret · a favour owed.

> [!info] GM only — Keeper Ulde
> Wants (surface): the toll in the costliest form. Beneath: a witness to the bell-house. If they stall: he prices what they did not offer. If they walk: a sluice opens one finger. If contradicted: agrees too quickly — the tell.

> [!abstract] Dramatic compass
> **Question:** what is the toll for — is Ulde collecting it, or paying it?
> **Rewarded here:** paying in truth.
> **Non-combat exit:** convince or pay; fighting closes the gate for the season.

If they derail: swimming the weir → the quiet water takes one carried item per swimmer, owner's choice.

### Scene 3 — The bell-house

> [!todo] Don't miss in this scene
> - [ ] `Spotlight → Iole:` the dive is hers.
> - [ ] The Wight will not strike whoever carries the ledger page — visible hesitation.
> - [ ] Wick: cutting the bell loose while it rings costs 1 Wick.

> [!quote] Read aloud
> The bell-house leans on four black stilts. Under the floor-grate a bell hangs down into the water, mouth beneath the surface, and the water around its lip is climbing in a slow coil, upstream.

**Visual cue V3** — GM-only show / change / stop: show with the bell-house description; change to blank when the party leaves or the described view no longer fits; stop on “weir” or any request. Brief only; fallback: blank.

Brief: Still of a leaning bell-house on four black stilts; beneath its floor-grate a bell hangs with its mouth underwater, water climbing in a slow coil around its lip. No figures, hands, markings, text or hidden history.

Asset note V3: brief drafted; no file generated, downloaded or inspected; provenance, license and allowed use unknown; playback not tested.

> [!example] Mechanics here
> Cutting the bell loose: two successes at threshold 8, underwater; each failure one wound from cold. Wight trait: strikes only whoever last spoke an untruth. Full block: [[Bell-Wight — stats]].

> [!info] GM only
> The bell was hung by Ulde's brother to drown the fen's debt-ledgers. The brother is dead under it; the Wight wears his hands. Seed: the knuckle-rhythm matches Ulde's coin gesture. Let Tobit find the guild-mark.

> [!abstract] Dramatic compass
> **Question:** the bell drowns the fen's debts along with the fen — who thinks that is a bad trade?
> **Rewarded here:** the argument itself, in character.
> **Non-combat exit:** the fight ends when the bell is drowned or cut loose, never by depletion.

If they derail: they let the bell ring → do not block it; next chapter becomes the flood chapter.
