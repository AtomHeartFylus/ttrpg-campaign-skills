# Cleo — Iole, the bell-diver

## Playstyle
Quiet until the scene goes underwater or under pressure, then decisive. Will volunteer for the
dangerous physical task before anyone asks.
Session 7 sharpened it: she will drop an agreed plan mid-scene if the water is right there — she
dived without the rope. Cost is not a deterrent for her; being made to wait is.

## Hooks
- The drowned sister — surfaced on the ferry in Session 7, in her own words.
- She owes Brenna one memory of home (the ferry toll, unpaid).
