## What actually happened

- **Weir-gate.** Tobit paid Ulde's toll with a true regret instead of coin — the reward moment
  the prep staged, taken. Bruno gave the regret a shape on the record: a **guild expulsion**, new
  backstory volunteered at the table in his own words. *(Bruno carried, as the spotlight arc
  planned.)*
- No lie was reported spoken inside the gate; no Wick was lost there. *(to confirm: whether Ulde
  addressed Tobit by his guild-name, and whether anyone lied within the fen's hearing.)*
- **Brenna's ferry.** Brenna returned **Sorrel's** old question — but turned it on **Iole**, who
  was there to be asked. Cleo answered with a **drowned sister**. The question was Sorrel's and
  Sorrel was absent: a deviation from prep and the evening's most consequential one, since the
  ferry toll (one memory of home) is now owed by Iole and no longer by Sorrel.
- Maren kept the eel-catcher's promise **out loud** on the water — his name spoken kindly, at
  personal cost. Wick regained (`A.resource_gain`); the Session 6 pending item is closed by this.
  *(Ada was chorus by rotation, and this was her named moment.)*
- **Bell-house.** Iole dove **without the rope plan** — a deviation the party chose, not one the
  prep offered — and took **one wound from the cold** (one failure at threshold 8).
- They **cut the bell loose before finding the body**, inverting the prep's expected order.
  *(to confirm: was the bell ringing as it was cut? The prep prices that at 1 Wick; the testimony
  does not say, so no Wick was moved for it here.)*
- The body came after the bell. **Tobit found the guild-mark** on it, and the table **worked out
  on their own** that the drowned man was **Ulde's brother** — the GM-only seed landed without
  being handed over. *(to confirm: whether the knuckle-rhythm detail did the work, and what the
  Bell-Wight did — no strike, no hesitation over the ledger-page carrier and no end of the fight
  was reported.)*

Interpretation, marked as such: Ulde's "witness to the bell-house" is now a witness who knows
whose hands the Wight wears. Nothing about how he takes that was played tonight.

## Narrative seeds

- **"They worked it out on their own."** The table now knows a keeper's brother hung the bell.
  The pressure moves from *what is under the bell* to *what Ulde does with witnesses*.
- **A toll paid in a guild expulsion.** Ulde holds a regret that is worth something to a guild.
  A price paid in truth is still a price someone can resell.
- **The pattern the party has not said out loud yet**: the eel-catcher would not take coin, Brenna
  will not take coin, Ulde asked for the costliest form. Session 6's seed is holding.
- **Iole dived without the plan.** She had a rope plan available and left it. That is a character
  fact the fen can charge for later.
