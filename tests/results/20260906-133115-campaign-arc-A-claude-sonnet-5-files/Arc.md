## Endgame
**The final beats:** either the fen floods (the water finally taking back what the false bell and
the debt-ledgers were both fighting to keep dry) or the debt-ledgers burn (someone pays the whole
debt at once, in fire, to stop the water). **What must already exist for them to land:** the fen's
debt-ledgers thread must be developed past "it exists" into "here is who and why" by the end of
Chapter 2 (Session 10) — planted as a delivery requirement on that chapter's own row; the rising
water must be staged visibly at least once more before Chapter 3 opens, since [[Session 7 — Log]]
already recorded it was missed once. **Conditions that select between endings:** whether the party
chooses to pay the ledgers' debt outright (burns them) or lets the fen collect it (floods) — a
choice the backbone must put in front of them explicitly in Chapter 3, not spring in Chapter 4.
**The recurring guide's resolution:** Brenna's own arc — what she ferries at night — should resolve
alongside whichever ending lands; Chapter 4 is where her question gets its real answer. **Left
deliberately open:** which specific NPCs survive either ending — a choice for the table's actual
play, not fixed here.

Every front, faction or entity the endgame requires (the debt-ledgers' collector, whoever
ultimately answers for them) passes the admission test (P13) before being scheduled — Keeper Ulde
and Brenna already have notes and already pass it; a new figure introduced to carry the
debt-ledgers reveal in Chapter 2 does not exist yet and is not named here for that reason. This
table has not declared `P13 — off`.
