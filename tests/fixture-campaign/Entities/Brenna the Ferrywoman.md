# Brenna the Ferrywoman

The campaign's recurring guide. Poles the only ferry below the weir; answers a question with a
toll — one memory of home, spoken, per answer.

## Why it is here
Someone must cross the water that the Charter will not price; Brenna ferries what the fen is
owed, both directions.

## What it represents
The fair price the toll-gate pretends to be — payment in kind, never in coin.

## The question it poses
What she ferries at night, and why she will not say until asked twice.

## Ledger of tolls owed to her
- Iole: one memory of home (Session 7, the drowned-sister answer).
